# Learning on the Pharmaceutical Production Floor

Industrial training connects classroom concepts with the practical controls used to manufacture medicines. The most valuable lesson is that a process is not just a sequence of machines: every stage must be controlled, documented and performed according to approved procedures.

## Wet granulation: a simplified flow

**Dispensing → Sifting → Dry mixing → Binder addition / granulation → Wet milling → Drying → Sizing → Blending → Compression**

The actual sequence and equipment depend on the product, validated process and approved batch manufacturing record.

## Equipment I wanted to understand

- **Rapid Mixer Granulator (RMG):** used for controlled wet massing and granule formation.
- **Multi Mill:** used for size reduction or wet/dry milling, depending on configuration.
- **Sifter:** separates material using specified screen sizes.
- **Fluid Bed Dryer (FBD):** dries granules using controlled airflow and temperature.
- **Blender:** supports uniform distribution of the blend before compression or filling.

## GMP mindset

- Follow the approved SOP and batch manufacturing record.
- Verify equipment status and cleanliness before use.
- Record activities at the time they are performed.
- Report deviations rather than silently correcting records.
- Never assume that two products use identical process settings.

## Reflection

Training is most useful when you ask why a parameter is controlled, what could go wrong if it changes, and where the evidence is recorded. This note is a learning summary, not a substitute for site procedures or product-specific instructions.
