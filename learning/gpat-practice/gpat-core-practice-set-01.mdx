# GPAT Core Practice Set 01

<Callout type="exam" title="Important">
All questions in this set are **Practice Questions**, written for revision. They are not represented as official GPAT previous-year questions.
</Callout>

Try each question before revealing the explanation. On the current article page, answers are placed after each question for straightforward self-checking.

## Pharmaceutics

**Q1. Which excipient is primarily added to reduce friction between a tablet and the die wall during ejection?**

A. Binder  
B. Lubricant  
C. Disintegrant  
D. Sweetener

**Answer: B — Lubricant.** Lubricants reduce die-wall friction during tablet compression and ejection. Magnesium stearate is a common example.

**Q2. Which equipment is commonly used to dry wet granules?**

A. Fluid bed dryer  
B. Friabilator  
C. Dissolution tester  
D. Tablet deduster

**Answer: A — Fluid bed dryer.** Heated, conditioned air fluidises the granules and supports moisture removal.

## Physical Pharmacy

**Q3. For an acidic buffer, when the salt concentration equals the acid concentration, pH is equal to:**

A. pKa  
B. pKb  
C. 7 always  
D. 14

**Answer: A — pKa.** Henderson–Hasselbalch gives pH = pKa + log(1); log(1) is zero.

## Pharmacology

**Q4. Enalapril belongs to which class?**

A. Angiotensin receptor blocker  
B. ACE inhibitor  
C. Calcium channel blocker  
D. Alpha-1 agonist

**Answer: B — ACE inhibitor.** Enalapril reduces angiotensin II formation by inhibiting ACE.

## Medicinal Chemistry

**Q5. Sulfonamide antibacterials act by inhibiting:**

A. Dihydropteroate synthase  
B. DNA-dependent RNA polymerase  
C. Dihydrofolate reductase  
D. Peptidoglycan transpeptidase

**Answer: A — Dihydropteroate synthase.** Sulfonamides are PABA analogues and competitively inhibit DHPS.

## Pharmacognosy

**Q6. Dragendorff reagent is commonly used to detect:**

A. Carbohydrates  
B. Alkaloids  
C. Proteins  
D. Fixed oils

**Answer: B — Alkaloids.** A positive reaction commonly gives an orange to reddish precipitate.

## Pharmaceutical Analysis

**Q7. If transmittance is 0.01, absorbance is:**

A. 0.01  
B. 1  
C. 2  
D. 10

**Answer: C — 2.** A = −log₁₀(0.01) = 2.

## Industrial Pharmacy

**Q8. Which qualification stage verifies that equipment is installed according to specification?**

A. OQ  
B. PQ  
C. IQ  
D. Process validation

**Answer: C — IQ.** Installation qualification documents correct installation against approved requirements.

## How to review your score

- **7–8 correct:** strong first-pass recall; move to application and numerical questions.
- **5–6 correct:** revisit the explanations and make a short error log.
- **0–4 correct:** rebuild the concepts before attempting a timed mixed set.

Do not treat one small set as a prediction of the actual exam. Use it to identify weak concepts and guide the next revision cycle.
