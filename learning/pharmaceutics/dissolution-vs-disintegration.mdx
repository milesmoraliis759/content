# Disintegration vs Dissolution

These two tests are related, but they do not measure the same event.

## The basic idea

**Disintegration** asks: does the dosage form break down into smaller particles under the specified test conditions?

**Dissolution** asks: how much of the drug substance enters solution over time under specified conditions?

A tablet can disintegrate without all of its drug dissolving. The drug usually needs to be in solution before it can be absorbed, although the relationship between in-vitro dissolution and in-vivo performance is formulation- and drug-dependent.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Feature | Disintegration | Dissolution |
|---|---|---|
| Main endpoint | Breakdown of dosage form | Drug released into solution |
| Typical observation | Tablets/capsules break into particles | Amount dissolved at specified time points |
| Main apparatus | Disintegration apparatus | Basket, paddle or another specified dissolution apparatus |
| Measures drug concentration? | Not usually | Yes, using a suitable analytical method |
| What it tells us | Physical breakdown behaviour | In-vitro release behaviour |

## Why both tests exist

Disintegration is a useful dosage-form quality test. Dissolution provides a more direct measurement of drug release into a medium and is important in development and quality control.

A passing disintegration result does not automatically establish a passing dissolution result. Similarly, dissolution results should be interpreted against the relevant product specification and validated analytical procedure.

## What can influence dissolution?

- Drug solubility and particle properties
- Formulation composition and manufacturing process
- Dissolution medium, pH and volume
- Apparatus and agitation conditions
- Temperature and sampling procedure

The exact method is product-specific. Always follow the applicable pharmacopoeial monograph or approved method.

## Easy memory line

**Disintegration = dosage form breaks.**  
**Dissolution = drug goes into solution.**

## Practice Questions

**Q1. Which test estimates the amount of drug released into a dissolution medium?**

A. Friability  
B. Disintegration  
C. Dissolution  
D. Hardness

**Answer: C.** Dissolution quantifies drug entering solution under defined conditions.

**Q2. A tablet disintegrates rapidly. What can be concluded about its dissolution?**

A. It must dissolve completely  
B. It must fail dissolution  
C. No definite conclusion; dissolution must be measured separately  
D. It has zero drug release

**Answer: C.** Disintegration and dissolution are different endpoints.
