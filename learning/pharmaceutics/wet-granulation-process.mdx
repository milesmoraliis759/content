## Start with the idea

Fine powders do not always flow or compress well on their own. Wet granulation uses a liquid binder to join small particles into larger granules before drying and compression.

**Granulation** converts fine powder particles into larger, multiparticle aggregates called granules. Wet granulation uses a granulating liquid, often containing a binder, to promote particle adhesion.

It is commonly selected when a formulation needs improved flow, reduced segregation or better compactability. It is not suitable for every API: moisture sensitivity, heat sensitivity and solvent compatibility must be considered.

<ProcessFlow title="Typical wet granulation sequence">
<ol>
<li>Dispensing and sifting of approved materials</li>

<li>Binder solution preparation and addition</li>
<li>Wet massing / granulation</li>
<li>Wet screening, if specified</li>
<li>Drying in a fluid bed dryer or another validated dryer</li>
<li>Dry sizing / milling</li>
<li>Blending with extragranular excipients</li>
<li>Lubrication</li>

</ol>
</ProcessFlow>

## 2. What happens at each stage?

| Stage | Purpose | Typical equipment / check |
|---|---|---|
| Dispensing and sifting | Correct weighing; remove lumps and foreign matter | Calibrated balances, sieve |
| Dry mixing | Distribute API and intragranular excipients | Rapid mixer granulator (RMG) or blender |
| Binder addition | Create liquid bridges between particles | Binder vessel, RMG |
| Wet massing | Build granules with suitable size and strength | RMG; endpoint is process-specific |
| Drying | Remove solvent to a defined endpoint | Fluid bed dryer (FBD), tray dryer |
| Sizing | Break oversized agglomerates and obtain suitable distribution | Multimill / sifter |
| Final blending | Distribute extragranular disintegrant or other ingredients | Blender |
| Lubrication | Add lubricant with controlled mixing | Blender |
| Compression | Form tablets at controlled settings | Tablet compression machine |

## 3. Key equipment in context

**Rapid Mixer Granulator (RMG):** combines dry mixing and wet massing in a bowl using an impeller and often a chopper. The impeller moves the powder bed; the chopper helps break lumps and control agglomerate growth.

**Fluid Bed Dryer (FBD):** passes conditioned air through a product bed, fluidising particles and supporting heat and mass transfer. Product temperature and moisture endpoint are controlled according to the validated process.

**Multimill:** uses a rotating blade assembly and screen to size material. Screen, speed, direction and feed rate are selected according to material properties and process instructions.

## 4. Critical process variables

- Binder type, concentration and addition rate
- Impeller and chopper speed / operating time
- Wet massing time and granulation endpoint
- Inlet air temperature, airflow and product temperature during drying
- Final moisture / loss on drying (LOD) target
- Mill screen and operating conditions
- Lubrication time and blend uniformity

A process parameter is not automatically a critical process parameter (CPP). Risk assessment and development data establish which variables require critical control.

## 5. What happens if the process is poorly controlled?

| Possible issue | Potential consequence |
|---|---|
| Too little binder or under-granulation | Weak granules, poor flow or friability concerns |
| Too much binder / over-wetting | Large dense granules, slow drying, altered dissolution |
| Inadequate drying | Excess moisture, stability or compression problems |
| Over-drying | Brittle granules, fines or poor compactability |
| Poor sizing | Broad particle-size distribution or segregation |
| Excessive lubrication | Reduced tablet bonding and potentially slower dissolution |

## 6. Wet vs dry granulation

| Feature | Wet granulation | Dry granulation |
|---|---|---|
| Granulating liquid | Used | Not used |
| Main mechanism | Liquid bridges / binder-mediated adhesion | Compaction and milling |
| Suitable when | Moisture and heat exposure are acceptable | Formulation is moisture- or heat-sensitive |
| Common methods | High-shear or fluid-bed granulation | Roller compaction or slugging |

## GPAT / industrial revision

- RMG: mixing and wet massing.
- FBD: drying.
- Multimill: sizing / milling.
- Blender: final blending and lubrication.
- Compression: converts the final blend into tablets.

**Do not memorise one universal machine sequence as a regulatory rule.** Follow the approved batch manufacturing record and site SOP.
